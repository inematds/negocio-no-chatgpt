In brief

As of November 10, 2026, ChatGPT is testing an official business registration feature. But physical stores and restaurants in Brazil still can’t register, and even businesses that can register will only show up if the AI understands what they do. negocio-no-chatgpt is a free kit for business owners who want to be found by AI: it builds a business profile for AI (ready-to-use text and a data block for your website) and rates your website with a list of fixes. It also includes step-by-step registration instructions based only on what OpenAI has confirmed. All you need is a computer with Python, a free program that runs the kit’s two files (the technical section explains how to install it).

What changed (November 10, 2026)

ChatGPT now has business registration

The feature is called “Businesses in ChatGPT” and is in beta. The dashboard where business owners manage their businesses is called Business Hub.

✋ Claim your business

The owner searches for the business, claims an existing profile or creates one, and proves ownership by phone, business email, a record on the website’s domain (your website administrator can handle this), or through Shopify if your online store is on Shopify. Identity verification comes next and may require an ID and business registration documents.

✏️ Manage your information

Website, description, category, logo, shipping and return policies. Businesses that sell products can submit a list with prices and inventory.

📊 View the summary

How many times the business was mentioned, how many clicks the website received, and the topics of conversations over the past 28 days. OpenAI says it doesn’t share people’s conversations.

Registering does not guarantee more visibility or sales: ChatGPT still chooses which businesses to recommend. You don’t need to advertise to register.

How AI finds your business

What helps AI understand you

ChatGPT search reads public webpages. Here are the four things this kit checks on your site:

🚪 The door is open to the bot

Every site has a file that says which bots can visit (robots.txt). If it blocks OpenAI’s search bot (OAI-SearchBot), your site won’t appear in search results. Changes to this file take about 24 hours to take effect. Blocking the training bot (GPTBot, which collects text to train AI) doesn’t remove you from search. The person who manages your site also needs to let the server accept OpenAI’s IP addresses.

📞 Contact details and city in text

Your phone number, email, and city should appear as actual text on the page, not just in an image or button. The kit’s checker only counts text: a phone number in a photo doesn’t count.

📝 Actual text

What you sell, where you sell it, your hours, and prices should be written on the page. In the kit’s check, a site that only appears after a program builds the page counts as empty.

🗂️ The data profile

A block of data hidden on the site, in a format search engines recognize (name, address, hours, prices, frequently asked questions). The kit’s profile creates this block, ready to paste in.

To be clear: OpenAI doesn’t say that a data block or better text will make your business “rank higher” in ChatGPT. The kit makes your site easier to read; it doesn’t guarantee a ranking.

Technical section

The kit: for anyone who wants to create a profile and check their site

The rest of this section covers the technical steps: installing, running the two programs, and registering. You don’t need this section to understand what came before.

What it is

Two small programs and a step-by-step guide

🗂️ ficha.py

It asks one question at a time (or reads an answer file) and generates ficha.md (text to paste), jsonld.html (a schema.org data block for the <head>), jsonld.json, and checklist.md. It never uses the internet.

🔍 checar_site.py

It reads an HTML file or URL (read-only, GET) and the site’s robots.txt/llms.txt. It gives a score from 0 to 100, with OK and FIX. Output is text or --json.

📋 CADASTRO.md

The step-by-step guide to Business Hub, based only on official sources (listed in FONTES.md), verified on November 13, 2026.

How it works

From answers to a score

Simple questions→ ficha.md + data block→ Paste into your site→ checar_site.py→ Score + FIX

Scoring: search bot allowed 30 · valid data block 20 · title and description 15 · contact info and city in the text 20 · at least 150 visible words 10 · llms.txt (optional) 5. The checker reads the HTML without running page scripts; it doesn’t check external CSS or whether OpenAI’s IP addresses are allowed on the server.

Requirements

A terminal and Python 3

No account, paid service, or extra package required. Enter commands in the terminal: the computer’s text-based window.

🖥️ Open the terminal

Windows: press the Windows key, type cmd, and press Enter (this opens Command Prompt). Mac: press Cmd + Space, type Terminal, and press Enter. Linux: Ctrl + Alt + T.

🐍 Python 3

Windows: install it from python.org (check “Add python.exe to PATH”) or from the Microsoft Store. Mac and Linux usually already have it. Check:

python3 --version
# on Windows: py --version

📦 The kit

Download the ZIP and extract it (right-click → Extract All). In the terminal, type cd followed by a space, drag the folder negocio-no-chatgpt-main that contains the ficha.py file into the window, and press Enter (Windows sometimes creates a folder inside another folder with the same name: use the inner one). If you use git:

git clone https://github.com/inematds/negocio-no-chatgpt
cd negocio-no-chatgpt
User guide · step by step

Step by step

On Windows, replace python3 with py in every command (for example, py ficha.py). Run all commands from inside the kit folder.

1

Test the kit

Run the tests (no internet required). The expected final message is todos os testes passaram.

python3 testes/run.py
2

Create your profile

Answer one question at a time; leave anything that doesn’t apply blank. For the type, enter the number or name (1 physical store, 2 restaurant, 3 local service, 4 online store, 5 online service); “padaria” works too. If an answer isn’t valid (type, phone, email, or website), the program explains why and asks again without losing your other answers. Separate products with commas. Enter business hours in this format: seg-sex 9h-18h, sáb 9h-13h. After the frequently asked questions, press Enter at a blank question to finish. The files are saved in the minha-ficha folder inside the kit folder.

python3 ficha.py
# business profile for AI generated in minha-ficha/
# Files: ficha.md, jsonld.html, jsonld.json, checklist.md
3

Or use an answer file

Copy the bakery example, open the copy in Notepad, replace the sample information with your real details, and generate the files without answering questions.

cp exemplos/padaria/respostas.json minhas-respostas.json
# on Windows: copy exemplos\padaria\respostas.json minhas-respostas.json
python3 ficha.py --de minhas-respostas.json --saida minha-ficha
4

Add it to your site

Paste the text from minha-ficha/ficha.md into your About page and profiles. Paste the contents of minha-ficha/jsonld.html inside your site’s <head>, using the same details shown on the page. Check robots.txt:

# robots.txt — lets OpenAI’s search bot in
User-agent: OAI-SearchBot
Allow: /

Don’t manage the site yourself? Send this to whoever does: “Can you paste the contents of this jsonld.html file inside the site’s <head> and check that robots.txt lets OAI-SearchBot in (User-agent: OAI-SearchBot / Allow: /)?”

5

Run your site through the checker

Use a URL (it fetches the page, robots.txt, and llms.txt) or a saved file. Try the kit’s examples first.

python3 checar_site.py exemplos/sites/bloqueado/index.html  # score 70/100
python3 checar_site.py https://seu-site.com.br
python3 checar_site.py https://seu-site.com.br --json
